A CLEARER PICTURE

Local AI & your privacy

PitchPilot sends your project details to the Ollama instance configured for this application. With Ollama running on your own computer, the brief does not need to be sent to a proprietary cloud AI provider.

  • PitchPilot does not intentionally persist client briefs on a server or database.
  • Generation is performed by the model shown in the status indicator.
  • Downloading Ollama and the model requires an internet connection. Local inference requires the model and Ollama to be available on your machine.
  • If you configure a remote Ollama host, that host receives your prompt. Check that host’s privacy and network configuration.
