# First-user test questions

1. What kind of freelance work do you do, and how often do you prepare proposals or quotes?
2. Was it clear what information to enter before generating a draft?
3. Did the generated proposal reflect the client brief accurately?
4. Which sections were useful, and which sections would you edit or remove?
5. Were the scope, timeline, assumptions, and pricing presented clearly enough to review with a client?
6. Did you feel in control of the final draft before copying or downloading it?
7. Would you pay for a tool like this? If so, what would make it worth paying for?
8. What feature would you want PitchPilot to add next?
9. Did anything feel confusing, untrustworthy, or difficult to use?
