# Example client brief

> Fictional example for demonstrating the input format. This is not a real
> client request.

Northstar Accounting is a small accounting firm looking for a responsive
five-page website: Home, Services, About, Resources, and Contact. They want a
contact form, service pages that explain bookkeeping and tax preparation, basic
on-page SEO, deployment, and handover documentation. They have not chosen a
hosting provider and will supply the final copy and logo. They would like the
site launched in about six weeks. They have not provided a budget.
